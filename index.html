Before you call

Five questions everyone asks, answered.

"Our data is a mess, it's too early for us"

There is no entry requirement. Cleaning up is the work, not a precondition for the work. Most projects start exactly there.

"How much does it cost?"

It depends on scope, and we give you a number before we start. After the diagnosis call you get a fixed quote, with no open-ended hours.

"How much of our team's time will it take?"

About two hours a week from one contact person, mostly to approve metric definitions and access. We do the rest.

"We already have Power BI and an analyst"

Good, then there is nothing to replace. A large part of our work is fixing and improving existing BI systems, not replacing them.

"What happens if you disappear?"

Your cloud, your accounts, the code in your repo, every model documented. At the end you get a handover pack and we delete our access.

"Are we too small for you?"

No. A well-built Excel system for a small business is a real project here, and often the right step before touching a data warehouse.
