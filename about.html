Who we are

Why a clinic, not another vendor.

Almost every organization we meet shows the same pattern: plenty of data, and no trust in it. Reports contradict each other, every department lives in its own spreadsheet, and big decisions run on gut feeling.

Data Clinic exists exactly for that. We work like a clinic: diagnose the state of your data, treat the root cause, and build an infrastructure you can rely on for the long run.

The result is a single source of truth: a clean data warehouse, dashboards people actually open every morning, apps that put the numbers in everyone's hands, and AI agents that answer in plain language.

FAQ

Common questions, honest answers.

How long does a typical project take?

A first dashboard goes live within a few weeks of kickoff. A full warehouse depends on scope. Either way, we agree on a fixed timeline before we start.

How much does it cost?

Depends on scope. After the intro call we send a fixed, closed quote. No surprises and no open-ended hours.

Do we need a data warehouse first?

No. If you don't have one, we set up BigQuery or Snowflake as part of the project. If you do, we work with what's there.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We work inside your cloud, with restricted read-only access. Your data never leaves your environment. An NDA is standard.

Can you support us after the project?

Yes. After launch we can stay on a monthly basis for monitoring, new metrics and improvements, for as long as it serves you.

Which BI tool do you recommend?

The one your team will actually open every morning. We work with Power BI, Tableau, Looker and Looker Studio, and recommend based on your stack and budget.
