OUR STORY

Why a clinic, and not just another agency.

After years of building BI inside startups and enterprises, one pattern kept repeating: every team had data, none of them trusted it.

Reports contradicted each other. The "marketing dashboard" said one number; the CFO's spreadsheet said another. Pipelines broke quietly on Sunday night and nobody noticed until Monday's standup.

We started Data Clinic because that's not a tooling problem - it's a craft problem. The right warehouse, the right modeling, the right monitoring, and the right senior specialist who owns it end-to-end.

The clinic metaphor isn't cute, it's literal. We diagnose, we treat, and then we monitor - so your data stays healthy long after the project ships.

FAQ

Common questions, honest answers.

How long does a typical project take?

A first dashboard ships in 2–3 weeks from kickoff. A full warehouse rebuild is 6–10 weeks. We always agree on a fixed timeline before starting - no open-ended retainers.

What does it cost?

Single dashboards start around $4–8k. Warehouse build-outs are $20–50k depending on scope. AI agents are priced per agent, with a small ongoing maintenance fee. We send a fixed quote after the scoping call.

Do we need a data warehouse first?

No. If you don't have one, we'll set up BigQuery (or Snowflake) as part of the project. If you already have one, we work with what you have - we don't migrate for the sake of migrating.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We work inside your cloud (your GCP/AWS/Azure project) with read-only service accounts scoped to specific schemas. Your data never leaves your tenant. NDAs and DPAs are standard.

Can we hire you for ongoing support?

Yes. After launch we offer a flat monthly retainer for monitoring, new metrics, and small features. Most clients keep us on for 3–12 months, then take it in-house with our handoff docs.

Which BI tool do you recommend?

Whatever your team will actually open every day. We're fluent in Power BI, Tableau, Looker, Looker Studio, and Metabase. We'll suggest based on your stack and budget - never based on commission (we don't take any).
