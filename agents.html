UNDER THE HOOD

How the agents stay grounded.

Every agent answer comes from your data, your definitions, and your business rules - not a generic LLM guessing. Here's how.

01
Your warehouse

Agents read from your BigQuery, Snowflake, or Redshift - never a third-party copy. Reads only, scoped tables.

→
02
Your metrics layer

Definitions live in dbt or your semantic layer. "Revenue" means the same thing across every agent and every team.

→
03
Cited answers

Every number an agent emits links back to the SQL, the model, and the row. No hallucinations, no opaque AI.

⚿

Read-only by default

Agents never write to your warehouse. Service accounts are scoped to the schemas you explicitly approve.

⚙

Bring your own LLM

Use OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Gemini, or your private Vertex/Bedrock endpoint - your data stays in your tenant.

↻

Audited & explainable

Every agent action is logged: question asked, SQL run, source rows, model used, latency, and approval chain.
